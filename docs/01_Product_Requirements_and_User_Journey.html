DailyBlog AI — Product Requirements & User Journey Blueprint

Comprehensive Product Requirements Document (PRD) and Multi-Role Operational Specification. Defines the multi-tenant architecture, human-in-the-loop governance gates, conversational AI authoring, and automated publishing pipeline for enterprise blog operations.

Specification Type: Document 1 (PRD) Target Architecture: Multi-Company Platform Status: Approved Product Design Security Class: Enterprise Isolated

1. Executive Product Overview Assessment & Product Core

DailyBlog AI is an enterprise-grade, multi-company AI-powered blog generation, human review, and automated publishing system. It is designed to eliminate the operational friction, creative fatigue, and high costs associated with maintaining an active, search-optimized company blog.

Unlike consumer-grade AI text generators that output unstructured copy directly to the public web, DailyBlog AI enforces a strict Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) governance model. The platform pairs AI topic generation and drafting capabilities with a rigorous multi-role editorial hierarchy:

Multi-Tenant Company Workspaces

Product Decision Strict tenant isolation ensures that company-specific data, knowledge, memory, conversations, blogs, and integrations are accessible only within the authenticated company's scope. Every company operates inside a private silo where its profile, knowledge base, tone-of-voice memory, prompts, blog drafts, review comments, and CMS publishing credentials are completely isolated.

Co-Pilot Editorial Collaboration

Product Decision Blog creation is not a black-box background script. It begins with intelligent topic suggestions, advances through structured drafting, and provides the human Editor with an active 15-minute intervention window and a collaborative conversational Edit Mode.

Enforced Editorial Review Windows

Product Decision An assigned Reviewer acts as the quality firewall. Time-gated decision windows prevent content from reaching external production without explicit sign-off, section-level improvement loops, or authorized rescheduling.

Automated Headless Publishing

Assessment Requirement Verified and approved blogs automatically dispatch to company CMS platforms (spearheaded by WordPress), complete with SEO metadata, structured headings, and automated post-publishing state reconciliation.

2. Problem Statement Assessment Requirement

Modern companies must maintain an active digital footprint to drive organic discovery, engage potential buyers, and reinforce brand authority. However, consistent content marketing presents acute operational bottlenecks:

  • Resource & Time Drain: Researching, drafting, optimizing, and formatting high-caliber articles every single day requires dedicated copywriting staff or expensive agency retainers.
  • Ideation Fatigue & Irregular Cadence: Marketing teams quickly exhaust immediate topic ideas, leading to erratic publishing schedules, broken SEO momentum, and audience drop-off.
  • Brand Voice Drift & Fragmented SEO: Content written across different personnel or unconstrained AI prompts suffers from inconsistent tone, missing semantic metadata, and poor keyword strategy.
  • Unregulated AI Hallucination Risks: Direct-to-publish automated bots risk publishing factual hallucinations, off-brand messaging, or embarrassing errors directly to company production websites.
The Solution Mandate: DailyBlog AI solves this by automating the heavy cognitive lift of trend research, structural outlining, and first-draft composition, while anchoring the process in company-specific context and requiring human oversight before any article goes live.

3. Product Objectives Assessment Requirement

The primary objectives defined for the DailyBlog AI platform are:

1. Daily Generation Automation

Deliver dependable, scheduled end-to-end daily content production without manual blank-page drafting.

2. Company & Industry Tailoring

Ground all topic suggestions and written prose in company-specific products, audience profiles, and verified knowledge.

3. Built-In Technical SEO

Generate search-optimized page titles, meta descriptions, semantic heading hierarchies (H1/H2/H3), and keyword targets.

4. Brand Voice Consistency

Maintain uniform tone, reading level, persona, and stylistic compliance across all generated articles.

5. Cost & Overhead Reduction

Compress content turnaround cycles from days to minutes, slashing marginal agency and freelance expenses.

6. Enforced Human Review

Equip human Editors and Reviewers with comprehensive approval, inline refinement, and feedback loops.

7. Scheduled Headless Publishing

Seamlessly dispatch approved content directly to company websites at predetermined daily publishing slots.

5. Primary User Roles Approved Product Design

DailyBlog AI strictly defines three company-facing roles. While a platform-level System Administrator exists conceptually for infrastructure maintenance, all operational business workflows occur strictly within these three roles:

1. Company Admin Executive Owner

  • Registers the company workspace and completes 4-stage onboarding.
  • Configures primary company metadata, logo, industry, and brand parameters.
  • Manages Reviewer onboarding, approvals, and credential revocation.
  • Configures integrations (e.g. WordPress API credentials) and notification emails.
  • Audits Editor lists, review statuses, accepted blogs, and conversation logs.
  • Restriction: Cannot alter the day-to-day publishing schedule owned by Reviewers.

2. Reviewer Editorial Gatekeeper

  • Requests access from Company Admin and receives secure temporary credentials.
  • Onboards, evaluates, and manages Editors within their organizational purview.
  • Owns and configures the company Weekly Publishing Schedule (Mon–Sun 24h).
  • Receives submitted drafts in an active notification queue.
  • Executes time-gated review decisions: Approve, Reject, Need Improvement, Reschedule.
  • Supplies section-level improvement feedback and audio/voice comments to Editors.

3. Editor Content Co-Pilot

  • Registers, links to company, and submits access requests to an active Reviewer.
  • Reviews daily AI topic suggestions and selects or supplies custom article topics.
  • Receives initial drafts within the 15-minute intervention control window.
  • Enters interactive Edit Mode: rewrites copy, chats with AI, uploads reference PDFs.
  • Defines and iterates on the company's versioned Global Blog Format with AI assistance.
  • Submits completed articles to Reviewers; views publishing schedule in read-only mode.

9. Reviewer Journey & Dashboard Specification Approved Product Design

The Reviewer operates as the quality firewall and publishing controller. The Reviewer guarantees brand adherence, editorial excellence, and schedule adherence:

Stage 1: Access Request & First-Login Onboarding

Reviewer inputs name, corporate email, and company ID, completing OTP and CAPTCHA. Admin accepts within ~24h. Reviewer uses a temporary password (valid 42h) and is forced to establish a permanent password on first login.

Stage 2: Publishing Schedule & Editor Management

Reviewer configures the weekly publishing calendar (Monday–Sunday 24h slots) and approves/deactivates incoming Editor access requests.

Stage 3: Active Review Queue Processing

Reviewer receives blog submissions in their action-item queue. Evaluates content quality against time-gated decision windows (Approve, Need Improvement, Reject, Reschedule).

Stage 4: Feedback Loops & Publication Clearance

If revisions are required, Reviewer records voice notes or section comments that inject directly into the Editor's AI chat thread. If approved, the post advances to automated WordPress publishing.

To prevent brute-force attacks and abuse of external email gateways:

13. Company AI Context Product Decision

The system shall maintain a tenant-isolated company context used to personalize topic suggestions and blog generation. All topic ideation, tone matching, and draft generation dynamically pull from this boundary:

Core Brand Identity

  • Company Details: Official brand name, size, operating regions.
  • Industry & Vertical: Domain niche, terminology standards.
  • Brand Voice: Authoritative, conversational, academic, or playful tone.
  • Content Guidelines: Words to avoid, formatting dos and don'ts.

Market & Commercial Posture

  • Products & Services: Feature sets, value propositions.
  • Target Audience: B2B enterprise leaders, B2C buyers, technical personas.
  • Marketing Goals: Lead generation, thought leadership, organic discovery.
  • Promotion Rules: Call-to-action (CTA) templates, link insertion rules.

Organizational Knowledge

  • Upcoming Projects: Launches, seasonal campaigns.
  • Strategic Partners: Ecosystem alliances, approved co-marketing notes.
  • Approved Knowledge: Whitepapers, fact-sheets, product catalogs.
  • Content Memory: Rolling history of previously published topics.
Absolute Isolation Mandate: The AI context of Company A must never bleed into Company B. Prompts, historical topics, reviewer comments, and uploaded documents are strictly partitioned by Company ID.

  • Introduction: Hook, problem articulation, thesis statement.
  • Structured Headings: H2 & H3 hierarchy organizing core arguments.
  • Main Content: Educational body sections, bullet points, data callouts.
  • Conclusion: Summary synthesis, closing thoughts, brand CTA.

SEO Optimization Standards Assessment Requirement

  • SEO Page Title: Optimized title with primary keyword Product Decision: 55–60 chars target.
  • Meta Description: Compelling search snippet Product Decision: 140–160 chars target.
  • Keyword Integration: Primary and semantic LSI keywords distributed naturally.
  • Search-Friendly Headings: Question-based and keyword-rich H2/H3 tags.
AI-Assisted Format Definition: Editors can define the company's future Global Blog Format via a guided AI dialogue. Crucial Product Rule: Defining or updating a Global Format does NOT trigger an immediate blog generation.

18. Editor-AI Journey Approved Product Design

The interaction between the Editor and the AI is structured as an ongoing, context-aware co-pilot relationship. Key operational rules govern this journey:

  • Single Continuous Thread: All activities for today's post—topic selection, draft generation, manual edits, AI instructions, and reviewer feedback loops—remain inside Today's Chat. New, disconnected chat sessions are strictly prohibited for the same cycle.
  • Multi-Modal Inputs: The Editor can instruct the AI via natural language chat prompts, file uploads (PDF, DOCX, TXT reference materials), and voice-to-text audio input.
  • Memory Retention: When asked to rewrite or expand a section, the AI retains the active blog text, user-provided edits, uploaded files, and company brand rules in its active context.

23. Need Improvement Workflow Approved Product Design

The "Need Improvement" action establishes a collaborative revision loop without requiring the Reviewer to rewrite the article:

24. Rescheduling Workflow Approved Product Design

Reschedule becomes available during the applicable 20–10 minute review window before the current scheduled publishing time. A reschedule operation remains permitted only while the next applicable scheduled publishing slot is at least one hour away.

  • Review Window vs. Next-Slot Cutoff: The 20–10 minute window (e.g. 3:40 PM – 3:50 PM for a 4:00 PM post) designates when the Reviewer can trigger the reschedule action. Separately, the rescheduled target time is constrained by the next scheduled publishing slot: a reschedule operation remains permitted only while the next applicable scheduled publishing slot is at least one hour away (e.g. if the regular next publishing slot is Tuesday 4:00 PM, the current blog may be rescheduled only until Tuesday 3:00 PM).
  • Cycle Continuity: Rescheduling keeps the same Editor-AI conversation thread and keeps the current blog active, changing only its publishing dispatch time without starting a new blog cycle.
  • Cycle Exclusivity: The system maintains strictly ONE active blog cycle per company. A rescheduled blog remains the active cycle until it is successfully published or enters an explicit publishing-failure state requiring recovery; a new daily cycle cannot start concurrently.
  • External Dispatch Failure: If a rescheduled publication attempt fails due to an external CMS outage, the failure is communicated to the Editor and Company Admin, and the current cycle remains unresolved until addressed.

30. Product-Level Security Requirements Approved Product Design

Credential & Token Protection

  • Zero plaintext password storage; all passwords hashed with enterprise cryptographic salts.
  • Third-party publishing credentials (WordPress Application Passwords) encrypted at rest.
  • Browser password autofill supported; system never retains plaintext in session state.

Destructive Action Safeguards

  • "Delete Company Account" requires explicit re-authentication and double confirmation.
  • User account deactivation revokes access tokens immediately while preserving audit logs.
  • Rate limiting on sensitive endpoints prevents credential stuffing.

31. Tenant Isolation Requirements Approved Product Design

DailyBlog AI strictly adheres to multi-tenant isolation. Company A and Company B operate in hermetically sealed virtual compartments:

Tenant Resource Isolation Guarantee & Security Boundary
Company Profile & Brand Rules Partitioned by unique Tenant ID. Context injections strictly bound to requesting tenant.
User Accounts & Roles Users exist strictly within their company hierarchy; no cross-company role inheritance.
Conversational Chat Memory AI conversation histories and temporary working memory are private to tenant and user.
Drafts, Blogs & Accepted Archive All blog entities are locked to tenant ownership; API queries filter by authenticated tenant.
Publishing Credentials Publishing credentials are encrypted at rest, scoped to the company's publishing integration, and never exposed in normal API responses or UI.

32. Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards Assessment & Product Core

The platform enforces a strict boundary between automated generation and publication authorization:

  • AI Role: The AI proposes topics, drafts text, suggests SEO tags, formats copy, and iterates on feedback. The AI cannot publish content autonomously.
  • Human Role: Humans select topics, review drafts, edit copy, provide subjective feedback, approve posts, and control publishing schedules.
  • Dual-Gate Defense: An article must survive both the Editor Control Window and the Reviewer Decision Window before reaching external publishing endpoints.
